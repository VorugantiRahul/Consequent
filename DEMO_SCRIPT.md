### Setup (Before Starting)
1. Open Chrome/Edge at: **`http://localhost:3000`**
2. The initial screen opens directly onto the **Meridian Systems (Case OL-2048)** operational workspace.
3. You can either click **"▶ Run Demo"** in the top bar to auto-play, or follow the 5 manual cues below:
